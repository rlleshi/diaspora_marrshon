# Pulse Community Links Design

## Goal

Connect engaged Pulse visitors with other volunteer projects for Albania without competing with the homepage's primary march actions.

## Placement

Add an unframed, full-width community row at the bottom of the homepage Pulse feature band. This keeps the invitation contextually close to movement participation while leaving the hero focused on the march.

## Content

- A short invitation to turn ideas into concrete work for Albania.
- A sister-site link to `flamingorevolution.eu` for discovering other initiatives.
- Localized Albanian and English copy.

## Behavior

- Open the external destination in a new tab with `noreferrer`.
- Track sister-site clicks as a Vercel Analytics event.
- Keep the link usable on narrow mobile screens.

## Verification

- Run TypeScript validation and a production build.
- Check the homepage Pulse band on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm both localized versions use the correct destination.
